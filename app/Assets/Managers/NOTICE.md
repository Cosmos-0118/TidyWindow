# Package manager icons

These upstream assets identify the respective package managers. Their names and logos belong to their respective owners; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

- WinGet: [Microsoft Windows Package Manager assets](https://github.com/microsoft/winget-cli/tree/master/.github/images/WindowsPackageManager_Assets), original `_256.png`.
- Chocolatey: [official Chocolatey icon](https://img.chocolatey.org/logos/chocolatey-icon.svg), linked from [Chocolatey's website](https://chocolatey.org/install).
- Scoop: [ScoopInstaller organization avatar](https://github.com/ScoopInstaller), downloaded from `https://github.com/ScoopInstaller.png?size=128`.

The assets are bundled locally and are not fetched at app startup.
